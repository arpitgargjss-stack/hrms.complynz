How roles are set
Everyone with a @complynz.com login lands as a team member. A manager role follows the reporting line automatically. HR admin and finance signatory are assigned by hand, to as few people as possible.
Logged access
Anything marked logged records who looked, what they looked at, and when. The audit log shows it to HR admins.
