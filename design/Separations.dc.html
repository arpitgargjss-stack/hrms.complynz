When HR accepts a resignation

The system sends the employee a confirmation letter, opens the exit checklist, lists assets to return, includes pending reimbursements in the final settlement, and can generate the experience and relieving letters once the checklist is clear.
A PIP is a written support plan with clear goals and review dates. The employee receives it, acknowledges it, and can respond. The outcome is decided at the final review, not by the system.
