1. Purpose

Complynz is committed to a workplace where everyone is treated with dignity and respect. This policy sets out how we prevent, prohibit and redress sexual harassment, in line with the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 and its Rules (the "POSH Act"). While the Act protects women, Complynz extends the protection of this policy to every person, regardless of gender or gender identity.

2. What counts as sexual harassment

Sexual harassment is any unwelcome act or behaviour of a sexual nature, whether direct or implied, including:

  • physical contact and advances;
  • a demand or request for sexual favours;
  • sexually coloured remarks, jokes or innuendo;
  • showing pornography or sexually explicit material, including by email, chat or social media;
  • any other unwelcome physical, verbal or non-verbal conduct of a sexual nature.

It also includes implied or explicit promises of preferential treatment, threats of detrimental treatment, interference with work, or creating an intimidating, hostile or offensive environment, when connected to such behaviour.

3. Workplace

"Workplace" includes our offices, client sites, work-from-home and remote settings, video calls, chat tools, offsites, business travel, company transport and any place visited in the course of employment.

5. Making a complaint

  1. The aggrieved person may file a written complaint with the IC within three months of the incident (or of the last incident in a series). The IC may extend this by up to three more months for good reasons.
  2. If the person cannot file in writing, any IC member will help them do so.
  3. If the person is unable to complain because of physical or mental incapacity or death, a relative, friend, co-worker or other person permitted by the Rules may complain on their behalf.

6. Conciliation and inquiry

  • At the request of the aggrieved person, the IC may first try conciliation. No monetary settlement can be the basis of conciliation.
  • Otherwise the IC conducts an inquiry following the principles of natural justice, giving both sides a fair hearing, and completes it within 90 days.
  • The IC submits its report to management within 10 days of completing the inquiry, and management acts on it within 60 days.
  • During the inquiry, the aggrieved person may ask for interim relief, such as a transfer, up to three months of paid leave, or a change in reporting line.

7. Outcomes

If the allegation is proven, action may include a written apology, warning, reprimand, withholding of promotion or increments, counselling, community service, deduction from salary towards compensation, or termination. If a complaint is found to be malicious or knowingly false, similar action may be taken against the complainant. A complaint that cannot be proven is not, by itself, malicious.

8. Confidentiality and no retaliation

The identity of the parties and witnesses, the contents of the complaint, the inquiry and the outcome are confidential under Section 16 of the Act. Breach of confidentiality is a disciplinary matter. Any retaliation against a complainant or witness is itself misconduct.

9. Awareness and reporting

All employees complete POSH awareness training at joining and once a year. The IC files an annual report with the District Officer and Complynz discloses the number of cases in its annual report as required by law.
